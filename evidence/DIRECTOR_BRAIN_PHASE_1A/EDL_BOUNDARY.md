# EDL Boundary (Frozen)

## Principle
DirectorDecision ≠ EDL.

- DirectorDecision: creative intent, semantic relations, uncertainty
- EDL/OTIO: execution representation (clips, tracks, time ranges)

## This phase

- No audio_offset field added to EDL
- No J_CUT field added to EDL
- No director intent embedded in EDL
- No MCP operation in EDL

## Future
